# Snip visual language

## Direction

Dark, quiet, and spacious. The page feels like a focused workspace with one warm atmospheric gesture behind the hero. Surfaces stay translucent and restrained so the coral-pink accent remains intentional.

## Tokens

- **Background:** `#0b0a0d`; deep plum-black canvas
- **Surface:** `#151319`; raised panel, with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` border
- **Text:** `#f7f3f1`; warm near-white
- **Muted:** `#a9a1a5`; secondary copy and metadata
- **Accent:** `#ff866f` coral to `#ed4d91` pink to `#9f78ff` violet
- **Font:** `"Avenir Next", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`
- **Type scale:** 0.78rem labels, 0.95rem body, 1.05rem controls, clamp(3rem, 8vw, 6.75rem) hero heading
- **Spacing:** 0.5rem base; 1rem control gaps; 1.5rem section gaps; 5rem hero breathing room
- **Radii:** 12px small controls, 18px surfaces, 999px hero input
- **Borders:** 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`
- **Shadows:** `0 24px 70px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.32)` on the links surface
- **Glow:** a fixed full-width top band with the warm accent gradient, blurred and pointer-transparent

## Snip mapping

- **Page header:** hero with the Snip name, bold headline, and muted subline.
- **URL form:** oversized pill-shaped chat input; the coral action button is attached at the right.
- **Result and error notices:** compact bordered message surfaces below the form; green is reserved for success and coral for errors.
- **Links table:** a single rounded, bordered surface with quiet row dividers and readable link metadata.
